# Executive Intelligence Briefing (EIB)

# Product Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap describes the long-term evolution of the Executive Intelligence Briefing (EIB) from an architecture and documentation project into a complete Executive Intelligence Platform.

Unlike a project plan, this document describes strategic direction rather than individual implementation tasks.

---

# Product Vision

Create the world's most trusted AI-assisted Executive Intelligence Platform.

The platform should reduce executive cognitive load by transforming fragmented information into concise, contextual, explainable, personalized intelligence.

The platform should become:

- Trusted
- Explainable
- Configurable
- Extensible
- International
- AI-assisted
- Human-centered

---

# Guiding Principles

Every roadmap decision should support at least one of the following:

- Better executive decisions
- Better intelligence
- Better explainability
- Better personalization
- Better architecture
- Better automation
- Better usability
- Better maintainability

# Success Metrics

The roadmap succeeds when:

- Repository is self-documenting.
- Architecture is stable.
- AI agents can contribute safely.
- New contributors can become productive quickly.
- Executive briefings are generated automatically.
- Intelligence remains explainable.
- Users trust recommendations.
- The platform scales without architectural redesign.

# Out of Scope

- Vendor lock-in
